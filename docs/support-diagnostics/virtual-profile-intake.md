# Virtual profile diagnostic intake

## QZ shows metrics but the receiving app cannot find a controllable trainer

### Provide first

- Confirm the physical bike's cadence and power update in QZ.
- Identify the devices running QZ and the receiving training app.
- Report whether Cycling/Running Sensor (Peloton compatibility) is enabled.
- Show the receiving app's pairing screen and the selected device role.

### If still unclear

- Compare discovery with that compatibility option disabled after restarting QZ.
- Share the QZ virtual Bluetooth settings and a scan of advertised services.

### Why it matters

- Sensor-only and controllable trainer profiles are different. A confirmed case restored trainer discovery after changing the compatibility option.
